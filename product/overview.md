# How it fits together

How the company portal and the workspace fit together.

## What it is

The public product guide covers the **company** portal and the **workspace**. The workspace is where a business unit links cloud accounts and works recommendations.

## Who it is for

Clients, operators, and support staff who need a map of the console before they click through a task.

## How to use it

1. Open [Company](company/README.md) for business units, users, roles, and company settings.
2. Open a business unit to enter the [workspace](workspace/README.md).
3. Open a cloud account for overview, cost, recommendations, and remediation.

## What to expect

A company can show **No active license** and still list its business-unit cards. The cards stay visible.

Company settings are for administrators.
