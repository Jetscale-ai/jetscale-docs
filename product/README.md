# Product

How the Jetscale company portal and workspace work.

Jetscale is organized so your team can find cloud waste, review recommendations, and prepare a remediation plan.

1. **Company** is your tenant: business units, users, roles, and company settings.
2. **Workspace** is the business unit and cloud account area: linked accounts, spend, governance, recommendations, and remediation plans.

You move through it in this order: company, business units, open a unit, cloud accounts, open an account, then overview, cost, resources when that item is in the menu, recommendations, and remediation.
