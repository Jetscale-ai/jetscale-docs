# Company

Business units, users, roles, and company settings.

The company portal is your tenant. From here you organize business units and administer people, roles, and company settings.

Company settings are for administrators. A role without that access does not see them.

## What you can open

- [Business units](business-units.md) — unit cards, the license banner, and adding a unit.
- [General settings](settings-general.md) — company name and contact fields.
- [Users](users.md) — the company directory and creating a user.
- [Roles](roles.md) — permissions, including remediation.
- [System settings](system-settings.md) — company configuration values.
- [Feature flags](feature-flags.md) — company overrides.
- [Profile](profile.md) — the avatar menu in the company portal.

Opening a business-unit card enters the [workspace](../main/README.md).
